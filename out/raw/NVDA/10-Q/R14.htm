Supplemental Financial Statement Information Supplemental Financial Statement Information
We refer to customers who purchase products directly from NVIDIA as direct customers, such as add-in board manufacturers, or AIBs, distributors, original design manufacturers, or ODMs, original equipment manufacturers, or OEMs, cloud service providers, or CSPs, AI model makers, and system integrators. Certain direct customers may use either internal resources or third-party system integrators to complete their build. Five direct customers accounted for 22%, 14%, 13%, 11%, and 10% of our accounts receivable balance as of July 26, 2026. Three direct customers accounted for 25%, 18%, and 13% of our accounts receivable balance as of January 25, 2026.
Payment from customers is generally due shortly after delivery of our products. In certain cases, for investment-grade customer purchases, we have and may in the future provide longer payment terms ranging from 90 days up to one year to assist customers with large data center builds depending on size.
