Recourse debt refers to debt that is recourse to our general assets. Non-recourse debt refers to debt that is recourse to only assets of our subsidiaries. The differences between the unpaid principal balances and the net carrying values are due to debt discounts or deferred issuance costs. As of September 30, 2026, we were in material compliance with all financial debt covenants.
Term Loan Facility
In September 2026, we entered into a senior unsecured delayed draw term loan facility with a commitment of $20.00 billion, maturing in September 2029 (the "Term Loan Facility"). The 3-year tenor is inclusive of an availability period of up to 18 months and is limited to ten draws. Undrawn commitments automatically reduce so that they do not exceed $10.00 billion at 12 months after closing, $5.00 billion at 15 months after closing and zero at 18 months after closing. Amounts repaid may not be reborrowed. Borrowings are available in U.S. dollars and may be used for general corporate purposes. Borrowed funds accrue interest at a variable rate equal to, at our election, Term SOFR or an alternate base rate, plus an applicable margin. The applicable margin will be based on the rating assigned to our senior, unsecured long-term indebtedness (or, in some cases, our issuer rating) (the "Credit Rating") from time to time. The fee for undrawn amounts is variable based on the Credit Rating and is currently 0.10% per annum.
Revolving Facilities
In September 2026, we entered into two senior unsecured revolving credit facilities with aggregate commitments of $10.00 billion (the "Revolving Facilities"), consisting of a 5-year revolving credit facility with a commitment of $8.00 billion, maturing in September 2031, and a 364-day revolving credit facility with a commitment of $2.00 billion, maturing in September 2027. The Revolving Facilities replaced our existing RCF Credit Agreement, which had a commitment of $5.00 billion and was scheduled to mature in January 2028. The 5-year facility contains two optional one-year extensions, and the 364-day facility includes a one-year term-out option for loans outstanding at maturity, in each case subject to certain conditions. Commitments under the Revolving Facilities may be increased by up to $4.00 billion in the aggregate under certain circumstances. The underlying borrowings may be used for general corporate purposes. The 5-year facility is available in U.S. dollars, euros and pounds sterling. The 364-day facility is available in U.S. dollars. Borrowed funds accrue interest at a variable rate equal to: (i) for dollar-denominated loans, at our election, Term SOFR or an alternate base rate; (ii) for loans denominated in pounds sterling, SONIA (the sterling overnight index average reference rate); or (iii) for loans denominated in euros, an adjusted EURIBOR (euro interbank offered rate); in each case, plus an applicable margin. The applicable margin will be based on the Credit Rating from time to time. The fee for undrawn amounts is variable based on the Credit Rating and is currently 0.10% per annum under the 5-year facility and 0.08% per annum under the 364-day facility.
Warehouse Agreement
In the first quarter of 2026, we entered into an uncommitted revolving Loan and Security Agreement (the "Warehouse Agreement") with a syndicate of banks. Under the Warehouse Agreement, we may borrow up to $1.50 billion in total principal amount which is secured by certain financing receivables and/or beneficial interests related to certain leased vehicles. Subject to extension in accordance with the terms of the Warehouse Agreement, the ability to draw under the Warehouse Agreement expires in March 2027, and the maturity date for borrowings is the earlier of the end of the underlying lease and loan terms or March 2034. Amounts drawn under the Warehouse Agreement bear interest at a rate equal to Term SOFR plus 0.65% to 1.00%. As of September 30, 2026, we have no borrowings outstanding under the Warehouse Agreement.
We are subject to certain conditions and limitations, including advance rate limits, a required reserve account, various performance triggers and excess concentration limits.
Automotive Asset-backed Notes
During the third quarter of 2026, we transferred beneficial interests related to certain leased vehicles into a special purpose entity and issued $709 million in aggregate principal amount of Automotive Asset-backed Notes, with terms similar to our other previously issued Automotive Asset-backed Notes. The proceeds from the issuance, net of debt issuance costs, were $706 million.
Energy Asset-backed Notes
In the second quarter of 2026, we transferred certain financing receivables into a special purpose entity (“SPE”) and issued $348 million in aggregate principal amount of Energy Asset-backed Notes, backed by these financing receivables. The proceeds from issuance, net of debt issuance costs, were $344 million. The SPE is wholly owned by us and is consolidated in the financial statements. The cash flows generated by these financing receivables are used to service the principal and interest payments on the Energy Asset-backed Notes and satisfy the SPE’s expenses, and any remaining cash is distributed to us. The SPE’s assets and cash flows are not available to our other creditors, and the creditors of the SPE, including the Energy Asset-backed Note holders, have no recourse to our other assets.
